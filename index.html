Good to know

A few things
you might wonder.

Still unsure? Your local blood service can give you advice for your situation.

Who can donate blood?+

Eligibility depends on your health, age, location, and other factors. A qualified healthcare professional or local blood service can tell you whether donating is right for you.

What happens after I register?+

Your registration is stored privately for an authorized coordinator to follow up. Submitting this form does not guarantee a match or a donation appointment.

Is this an emergency service?+

No. For an urgent medical need, contact your local emergency service and the hospital blood bank directly. Do not wait for a response to this form.

Will my details be public?+

No public donor list is provided by this website. Your information should only be accessible to authorized service coordinators under the project's Firebase security rules.
